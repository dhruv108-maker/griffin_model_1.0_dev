# Palette's UX & Accessibility Journal

## 2025-05-18 - Disabled Form Action Clarity & Accessibility
**Learning:** Disabled action buttons in complex form or upload workflows can frustrate users if there is no explicit visual hint or accessible screen reader association (`aria-describedby`) explaining why the button cannot be pressed.
**Action:** Always provide helper text and `aria-describedby` alongside `disabled` submit/action buttons to clearly communicate remaining requirements.
